# Brag Plan: Bible Diaries Web Application

## What is this app?
A spiritual journaling and community reflection web application built with Next.js 16, React 19, Supabase, and OpenAI Content Moderation, empowering believers worldwide to document their spiritual walk, share scripture reflections across categories, and connect in uplifting daily fellowship.

## The angle
Quiet, refined spiritual depth and modern web craft. Instead of generic hype, showcase the authentic experience of daily faith reflection: scripture-centered journaling, AI-moderated sanctuary integrity, category filtering, and real-time community fellowship.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Pure white canvas (#FFFFFF) with an ambient soft sky-blue glow. The brand mark "Bible Diaries" resolves cleanly alongside the subtitle "Where Believers Reflect & Connect" and a verse preview. A calm, inviting opening that earns the viewer's attention.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Authentic Scripture Reflection Card**: Proverbs 3:5-6 "Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding..." by author Grace Mwangi, categorized under "Wisdom", with interaction metrics (42 Likes, 8 Notes, Saved).
- **Spiritual Category Filtering**: Interactive row of category buttons — Hope, Faith, Love, Strength, Gratitude, and Wisdom — with Wisdom active.
- **AI Sanctuary Moderation**: Seamless real-time moderation flow screening submissions before community release to protect uplifting fellowship.

## Outro / punchline
"Bible Diaries. Where Believers Reflect & Connect. Aligning hearts everywhere."

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Discover inspiring reflections in the Global Feed.
2. Filter: Switch categories to "Wisdom" and explore scripture entries.
3. Fellowship: Prayerful encouragement with like and note reactions.
