## Editorial scope

The legal centre now contains substantive guidance rather than five short document summaries, with direct links to the full documents at the top. Terms cover the website/app distinction, consent, content ownership, publication permission, quotations, conduct, messages, images, removal, restrictions, availability, future transactions, feedback and mandatory rights. Privacy covers sensitive faith-related disclosures, purpose limits, requests, provider processing, sessions, retention and incidents. Cookie and waitlist documents explain the actual storage and consent boundaries.

Support provides practical troubleshooting for forms, duplicate entries, email expectations, rate limits, PC/mobile access, accounts, recovery, missing content, profile settings, messages, privacy requests and the administrator dashboard. Safety expands protection of personal information, pressure, impersonation, money requests, links, unwanted contact, spiritual coercion, children, evidence, reports and shared devices. Community and security guidance address the related conduct and technical boundaries.

Paragraphs remain separate in the rendered page. Contents lists scroll within the viewport, with narrower layouts for mobile. Official background-reading links are provided on the relevant legal/privacy documents. The shared page renderer also renders the dedicated waitlist route, so the longer notice uses the same accessible structure and navigation.

## Accuracy boundaries

- Launch availability, store order and PC access follow the existing product status; no live download, release date or Windows installer is invented.
- Waiting-list email ownership remains unverified and confirmation delivery is not claimed. Administrator email verification is explained separately.
- Subscriber lists remain outside the aggregate dashboard, and no new collection, upload, messaging or reporting feature is introduced by these content changes.
- Existing browser-app authentication storage is distinguished from administrator cookies and local privacy preferences.
- No unimplemented in-app blocking button, emergency response, guaranteed recovery, support response time or end-to-end encryption is promised.
- Removal requests explain independent copies and applicable retention rather than promising instantaneous erasure from every system.

## Background sources and publication review

Official Information Regulator guidance and complaint resources, and South African Government pages for the Consumer Protection Act and Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, informed the legal-context check. The text is original service-specific guidance; it does not reproduce the statutes or present the operator as certified by a regulator.

The operator/contact identity and existing app minimum age are carried forward from repository content. Before public deployment, the operator should confirm its exact legal identity, contact arrangements, actual provider contracts and retention procedures, child/sensitive-information requirements and the applicability of each policy to the released service. The expanded drafting is not an independent legal opinion or a replacement for an appropriate legal review. No registration number, business address, guaranteed statutory outcome or court jurisdiction was invented.

Browser visual QA and live infrastructure verification remain outside the checks performed in this environment. Long-document performance should be measured on the deployed host rather than inferred from word count.
