# Bible Diaries

Bible Diaries is an upcoming spiritual journaling and community reflection platform from T.S Industries.

Purpose: reflect on Scripture, write and share spiritual reflections, explore hope, faith, love, strength, gratitude and wisdom, bookmark entries and connect with fellow believers.

Release: Google Play and Huawei AppGallery are planned first. Apple App Store is planned for later. No release date is confirmed. This marketing website does not provide an app download or create app accounts.

PC: a separate browser companion exists in the project. Its public address and availability will be announced when ready. There is no promised Windows desktop installer, offline mode or exact mobile/web feature parity.
